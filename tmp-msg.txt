An area states what it is billed, and a metered rate opens at a peso

Two faults the office found by reading its own screen, and one of them was mine.

THE AREA ROWS READ NOUGHT. The three canonical areas rendered the stored amount, so an area nobody had priced showed
P0.00. The office called that a bug and it was right: nought in that column does not mean free, it means the area follows
the market's own daily stall fee, which is what the fee rule resolves to. The row now states the figure the area is
actually billed - its own where the office has stated one, the market's otherwise - and where the market itself has no
stated fee it reads "Not set" rather than inventing a figure. The note under the group already said as much; the value
now agrees with it.

A METERED RATE OPENED AT NOUGHT. The office asked for a peso instead, and the difference between where that peso goes was
put to it plainly: on the FORM it is an offer the office confirms; in the RESOLVER it is this platform pricing a utility,
which is the exact fault found when Madrid was billing Cantilan's per-kilo fee. The office chose the form, so the figure
lives in Client/Services/UtilityRateSuggestion.cs, which says in its own remarks that a resolver, a report, a bill or a
settlement asking it for a figure would be that fault in a new place. It is seeded when the office presses Edit rates,
because that press is the office asking for a form and a form is the only place a suggested figure belongs. It sits in the
field where the office can see and change it, and it becomes a rate on Save like every other figure there.

One consequence stated rather than buried: Save writes every changed rate, so saving now also states a peso for a metered
rate that had none. The field shows the figure and one line says what it is doing, so nothing is hidden, but Save does
more than it did.

Twenty-one tests, both directions injection-proved: the old P0.00 display fails two of them, and removing the suggestion
fails two more. The suggestion reaches only the two metered keys, never a rate the office has already stated, never a fee
that is not metered, and nothing is sent to the office's record merely by opening the form.

Also recorded, because the office's reasoning for keeping the section metering rested on it: onboarding does NOT set
utilities. It carries a custom area's name and nothing else. Keeping the block is still the right call, for a better
reason - Facility Configuration is the only place a municipality that is not Cantilan can state that an area's stalls are
usually metered.
