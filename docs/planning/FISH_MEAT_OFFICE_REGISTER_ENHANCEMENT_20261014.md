## Scope and office evidence
Reference: MEEDO Fish Retailing business table and docs/evidence/2026-10-06_meedo_office_terminal_fish_vendor_monthly_income_clarification.md.
The office reference includes business name, last/first/middle/extension names, sex, location/address, New/Renew, tax year and nature of business. The handwritten values in the right-hand margin are monthly report/reference totals according to that clarification, **not** rates, customer amounts due, individual transactions, or proof of a new canonical Collection. Their exact month and connection to transactional evidence still require office confirmation. None has been seeded or posted.

## Changes
- New **optional** office-register fields on FishMeatVendorRegistration and source-native DTOs: LastName, FirstName, MiddleName, NameExtension, Sex, NatureOfBusiness. Nullable schema migration: FishMeatOfficeRegisterFields20261014. Unavailable historical facts stay null, with no invented values. Sex is supplied by office records, never inferred from name. Renewals carry office details forward; changing them during renewal is deferred because the existing renewal command does not accept edited office profile facts.
- The standard registration drawer captures the optional fields with a suggested (editable, not authoritative) nature-of-business input. Fish or Meat remains an independent explicit vendor-type choice.
- From NPM occupants: the initial creation step now leads to the proper registration form, without the old preliminary generic note. **The backend requires verified NPM association evidence**, so the required office link reference is moved into the registration form and its submission is disabled until supplied. Linking an existing registration retains the required evidence input. Historical/area mismatch, possible duplicate and Head-only controls are preserved.
- Import Fish/Meat vendors uses the existing civic-blue import hero. Its editable register and header-only template include office paper fields. A CSV with First/Last can derive a display name only when a display name is missing; no vendor **type** is ever inferred from fish/meat nature of business, and ambiguous paper columns do not create a vendor type. Per-row tax-year column, where present, must match selected tax year. Unknown monetary columns are ignored and never submitted; current server preview/duplicate review and idempotency rules remain.
- New read-only **Office register** route at /operations/fish-meat-vendor-fees/office-register (linked from main operation and import success). Civic-blue summary, monthly/registration-year selectors, Fish/Meat filter, search, printable office-style identity/business rows, and independent Vendor Fee / Weight & Measure columns, with walk-up vendor fees separately disclosed. Uses the existing authorized tenant-scoped ManageRegistrationsAsync reader, not inferred sums from the paper. The report is not the complete official Monthly Income report; the existing /report remains available.
- No collection posting logic, OR issuance, weighing, rental, billing rate, tenant policy or financial summary classification is changed.
