# MEEDO reply on the late penalty rate — 2026-11-23

**Source:** Direct chat with the Cantilan MEEDO office, relayed by the project owner on 2026-11-23. The owner states no further question can be sent.
**Authority:** Direct Cantilan office statement, for the INF-4D panel compliance (Maligsa, Flores: interest and penalties).

## Question (owner)

Whether there is a late penalty on monthly stall rent, and how much: a percentage of what is unpaid or a fixed amount; charged once or every month; with any limit; the due date and grace days; and the ordinance it comes from.

## Reply (MEEDO, as relayed by the owner)

> ang penalty is 25% then an surcharge kay 20% per month

Reading used by StallTrack (owner to correct if the wording means otherwise):

1. **Penalty:** 25% of the late rent, charged once.
2. **Monthly surcharge:** relayed as 20% a month. RA 7160 (Local Government Code), Sec. 168, lets a sanggunian impose a surcharge of up to 25% and interest of up to 2% a month, for at most 36 months, so 20% a month is likely 2%. It is not entered until the ordinance confirms the figure.
3. **Ordinance, due date, grace days:** not named. The due date stays the last day of the month with no grace days, the StallTrack defaults, under which an unpaid month is Delinquent the morning after it ends (IA-019).

## What StallTrack does with it

- Cantilan's Head records, on **Settings → Billing policy**: Percent of the unpaid rent, 25, not charged again each month, no ceiling, with this statement as the authority. Unpaid months count from 1 December 2026, so the first charge can fall on 1 January 2027, on December rent still unpaid.
- Billing Policy holds one penalty rule. A one-time penalty plus a capped monthly surcharge is a follow-up, made when the ordinance is in hand.
- Nothing is charged on months before 1 December 2026.
